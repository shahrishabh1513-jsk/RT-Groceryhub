Our Mission

Fresh Food Should Be a Right, Not a Privilege

We started GreenBasket because we were tired of the same problem: supermarkets selling day-old produce at inflated prices, while small-scale farmers struggled to find buyers for their freshest harvests.

By connecting farms directly to your doorstep and delivering within 30 minutes, we eliminate the middlemen, keep prices honest, and ensure you get genuinely fresh food — every single time.

Today, we partner with 200+ certified organic farms across India, serve 50,000+ happy households, and deliver over 5,000 orders every day.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Three principles guide everything we do — from sourcing to delivery.

🌿

Radical Freshness

We source produce within 24 hours of harvest. Our dark stores receive deliveries twice daily. If it's on our platform, it's genuinely fresh — or we refund you, full stop.

🤝

Farmer First

We pay our farm partners 30% more than wholesale market rates. Happy farmers grow better food. We believe in fair trade that benefits the entire food chain.

🌍

Planet Positive

100% plastic-free packaging, carbon-neutral delivery fleet, and zero food waste policy. Every GreenBasket order contributes to a healthier planet.

Our Journey

How GreenBasket Grew

2020

🌱 The Seed is Planted

Founded by Aditya Kumar and Meera Nair in a small garage in Mumbai. GreenBasket starts as a WhatsApp-based vegetable delivery service with 3 farm partners and 50 customers.

2021

📱 Going Digital

Launched our first website and mobile app. Expanded to 20 farm partners, 500+ products, and 2,000 customers across Mumbai. Secured ₹2 Cr seed funding.

2022

🏪 Dark Store Expansion

Opened 5 dark stores across Mumbai and expanded to Pune. Introduced 30-minute delivery guarantee, artisan brands, and pantry staples. Team grew to 120 people.

2023

🌆 Multi-City Launch

Expanded to Bengaluru, Delhi, and Hyderabad. Crossed 25,000 customers and ₹50 Cr in annual revenue. Launched premium organic and imported product range.

2024 – Now

🚀 Scaling New Heights

50,000+ customers across 6 cities, 200+ farm partners, 5,000 daily orders. Launched our loyalty programme, Razorpay secure payments, and the industry's first 20-minute delivery in select areas.
